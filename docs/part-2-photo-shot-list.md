source once, under the matching `public/images/` directory. Use lossless masters
outside the repository and optimized WebP/AVIF for the site.

## Quality floor

- Hero: ideally 2400 px or wider after the intended crop.
- Product card: ideally 1000 px or wider, consistent orientation.
- Never introduce visible fake names, signage or unsupported facility claims.
- Use consistent warm cinematic lighting without heavy artificial sharpening.
- Add informative alt text once photos are finalized.
